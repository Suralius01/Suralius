Game progress

Casefile Daily keeps your puzzle progress, notebook entries, evidence pins, hints, results, XP, streaks, and preferences on your device. It uses these records to resume cases and show your detective record. If a save cannot be read, the game may keep a local recovery copy.

The game works without an account. The publisher keeps game progress in local storage on your device and operates no separate analytics or cloud-save service. Google Mobile Ads handles the advertising data described below.

Advertising

The Android game uses Google Mobile Ads, also called AdMob, to show occasional ads between cases and optional rewarded ads for extra hints. Its advertising and consent systems can process IP addresses, approximate location, device or advertising identifiers, interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. Google encrypts the data its Mobile Ads SDK sends in transit.

The game requests non-personalized ads. Where applicable, it presents Google’s consent options before requesting ads. You can find the available controls under Settings → Privacy & advertising → Manage advertising privacy. Ad availability may depend on your choices. You can solve every case using its evidence.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information for details about Google's handling of advertising data and your controls.

Reminders

If you enable daily reminders, the game requests notification permission and schedules reminders locally. You can turn them off in the game or in Android's notification settings.

Sharing and backups

You choose where to send a shared result or exported backup. Shared results can include the case title, completion status, hints used, and time spent. A backup contains your game history and preferences. The destination you choose handles the copy you send.

Support messages

If you contact the publisher, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use this information to answer your message and investigate the issue you report.

We limit access to people handling your request. We keep support messages, bug reports, and attachments while we work on the issue and any follow-up. We delete them when they are no longer needed.

Use the contact email above to ask about or request deletion of information you sent to the publisher.

Deleting saved data

Reset Progress clears the active game record and keeps preferences. You can remove local game data by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings. Android cloud backup is disabled in this build. Copies you export stay wherever you save or share them. Google manages the retention of data processed by its advertising services under its own policies.

Intended audience

Casefile Daily is intended for adults aged 18 and over. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has shared personal information with us, contact the email listed in this policy. We will review your request and delete information we hold when appropriate.

Policy updates

This page may be updated as the game or its services change. The effective date above identifies the current policy. You can send privacy questions to the contact email listed above.
