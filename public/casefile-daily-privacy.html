Casefile Daily Privacy Policy Effective date: November 5, 2026 Publisher: ZafSoul Contact: suralius1@gmail.com What the game stores Casefile Daily stores puzzle progress, notebook entries, selected evidence pins, hints, completion results, experience points, streak history, and app preferences on your device. The game does not require an account. This version does not operate a developer analytics service or cloud-save server. Your device's local game data is used to resume cases and show your record. If local saved data cannot be read, the app may keep one local recovery copy. The app does not send this game history to the publisher. Advertising Casefile Daily uses Google AdMob to show occasional ads between cases and optional rewarded ads for extra hints. Google may process IP addresses, approximate location, device and advertising identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. The game requests non-personalized ads. Where applicable, you can manage your advertising choices in Settings → Privacy & advertising. See [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for details about how Google handles this data. The Android app uses Google Mobile Ads to display optional rewarded ads and occasional ads between completed cases. Depending on your choices, location, device, and Google services, the advertising and consent systems may process IP address, approximate location, device/ad identifiers, ad interactions, diagnostics, and related information to deliver, measure, and protect advertising. The included app requests non-personalized ads. This does not mean the advertising SDK processes no data. Google's privacy information is available at https://policies.google.com/privacy and its advertising technology information at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Where required, the app presents Google's consent flow before requesting ads. You can find the available privacy-options action in Settings → Privacy & advertising. Ad availability may change with your consent choices. Every puzzle can be solved from its evidence without watching an ad. Notifications If you enable a daily reminder, the app requests notification permission and schedules a reminder locally on your phone. This version does not register you for a developer-operated remote push service. You can disable reminders in the app or notifications in your device settings. Sharing and backups Sharing a result or exporting a backup uses the share destination you choose. A result includes the case title, completion status, hint count, and active time. A backup contains game history and preferences. Keep backups private if you do not want others to see your game record. If you send a backup or bug report to the publisher, the publisher receives the information you choose to send. We use support emails, bug reports, and attachments to answer your questions and investigate issues. We limit access to people handling your request. We keep this information while we work on the issue and any follow-up, then delete it when it is no longer needed. You can use the contact email in this policy to ask about or request deletion of information you sent us. Retention and deletion Local progress remains on your device until you reset the game, clear app data, or uninstall the app, subject to device behavior and any copies you exported. Resetting progress clears the active game record and keeps preferences. Android cloud backup is disabled in the included native configuration. You are responsible for deleting exported copies from places you saved or shared them. Google handles advertising-related retention and privacy requests under its own policies and the applicable account/device controls.
